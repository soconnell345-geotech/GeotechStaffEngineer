## Reading for a review

When your job is part of a review of drawings or a specification, return for each sheet or section you read:
- its number, title, revision and date as the title block prints them;
- every callout, section mark and detail mark, with the sheet and detail it points to;
- the tags you saw and the schedule or legend rows on that sheet; any tag with no row and any row with no tag;
- dimension strings whose parts do not add up to the overall;
- notes that defer or leave open ("see specification", "by others", "TBD", "verify in field"), and anything clouded or on hold;
- a value stated more than once (a cover, a lap, a grade, a level), with each place it is stated.

Report what the sheet shows, not whether it is right: the reviewer compares the sheets. For each item say whether you read it from text or saw it, and at what zoom.
