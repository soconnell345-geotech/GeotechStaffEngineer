# Drawing notes against the project manual: North Yard Annex, issue 1

## Conflict

1. **S-001 note 6: footing concrete strength.**
   - The drawings say "FOOTINGS 25 MPa".
   - Section 03 30 00, 2.2 makes Class A (footings) **30 MPa** at a maximum w/cm of 0.50.
   - The other two strengths in the note agree with the manual: slabs on grade 30 MPa is Class B, and columns and suspended slabs 35 MPa is Class C.

## Reference that does not resolve

2. **G-002 note 6:** "FIRESTOPPING AT RATED PENETRATIONS: SEE SECTION **07 84 00**".
   - 07 84 00 is not in the manual.
   - The table of contents lists only 01 33 00, 03 30 00, 04 20 00, 07 92 00, 08 11 13, 08 71 00 and 09 91 00, and states "Sections not listed here are not part of this project manual."

## References that resolve

- **G-002 notes 4, 5 and 7 to 11:** 03 30 00, 04 20 00, 07 92 00, 08 11 13, 08 71 00, 09 91 00 and 01 33 00 are all in the table of contents.
- **A-601 door notes 2 and 3:** 08 11 13 and 08 71 00 are both listed.
- **Hardware sets:** the sets used on A-601 (HW-1, HW-2 and HW-4) are all defined in 08 71 00, 2.1.

## Related to the manual

- 08 71 00, 2.1 says the door schedule names the hardware set for each door. Door 109 (shown on A-101 and A-401) is not in the schedule, so as drawn it has no hardware set.

## What I could not check

- The copy of the manual I have contains the text of 01 33 00, 03 30 00 and 08 71 00 only.
- The other four referenced sections (04 20 00, 07 92 00, 08 11 13 and 09 91 00) appear in the table of contents, but their text is not included, so I could not check the drawings against their content.
