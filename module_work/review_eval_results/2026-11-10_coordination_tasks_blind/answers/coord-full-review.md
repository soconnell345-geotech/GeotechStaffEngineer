# Coordination review: North Yard Annex drawings, issue 1 (15 sheets)

This review checks the drawing set against the Project Manual (Issue 1) and Geotechnical Memorandum G-24-031.

| # | Sheet | What is wrong |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | G-001 | The sheet index lists **A-202 INTERIOR ELEVATIONS**, but there is no A-202 in the set. The set has 15 sheets ("SHEET n OF 15") and the index lists 16. Add the sheet or delete the entry. |
| 2 | G-001 | The index titles **S-201** "ROOF FRAMING PLAN". The sheet is the SECOND FLOOR FRAMING PLAN; S-202 is the roof framing plan. |
| 3 | G-002 | Note 6 refers to **Section 07 84 00** (firestopping). That section is not in the project manual: the table of contents lists only 01 33 00, 03 30 00, 04 20 00, 07 92 00, 08 11 13, 08 71 00 and 09 91 00, and excludes all others. |
| 4 | A-101 | Detail callout **7/A-501** at the east wall points to a detail that doesn't exist. A-501 has details 1 to 6 only. |
| 5 | A-101, A-401 | **Door 109** (lobby into the core / Stair 1) is tagged on both plans but is **not in the door schedule** on A-601. It therefore has no size, frame, rating or hardware set, and 08 71 00, 2.1 relies on the schedule to name the set. |
| 6 | A-102 | Section callout **2/A-302** points to a sheet that doesn't exist. The same cut on A-101 is 2/A-301. |
| 7 | A-601 | **Door 204** (OFFICE, HW-2) is scheduled but **not shown on A-102**, which tags doors 201, 202, 203, 205 and 206 only. No door is drawn at that position. |
| 8 | A-301 | The second floor is shown at **EL. 104.50** in both sections. A-201 and S-201 (T.O. slab) give **EL. 104.20**, and the A-301 level line is drawn at the 104.20 height. |
| 9 | A-401 | Grid B to C is dimensioned **7500** in both enlarged core plans (clouded, rev 1). A-101, A-102, S-101, S-201 and S-202 show **7200**, and the A-401 grid lines are drawn 7200 apart. Correct the dimension, or revise every plan if the grid really changed. Rev 1 is described as "REVISED CORE LAYOUT", which does not describe this change. |
| 10 | S-001 | Note 2 gives an allowable net bearing pressure of **200 kPa**. Memo 3.2 gives **150 kPa**, which limits settlement to about 25 mm. Revise the note and recheck the footing sizes. |
| 11 | S-001 | Note 3 sets the bottom of exterior footings **1.2 m** below finished grade. Memo 3.3 requires at least **1.5 m**. As drawn, the footings bear at about El. 97.5, roughly 2.2 m deep, so only the note needs correcting. |
| 12 | S-001 | Note 6 gives footing concrete at **25 MPa**. Section 03 30 00, 2.2 specifies Class A (footings) at **30 MPa**. |
| 13 | S-101 | Footing **F4** at C/2, drawn about 2600 mm square, is **not in the footing schedule** on S-501, which lists F1 to F3 only. |
| 14 | S-001 | Note 9 (slab on grade) leaves out memo 3.6: the 150 mm granular base is to be placed over the dense sand, **with all fill removed**. The fill is about 0.9 m thick. |
| 15 | S-001, S-101 | No note requires footings to bear on the dense sand below all fill (memo 3.1), or requires the geotechnical engineer to **inspect each footing subgrade** before concrete is placed (memo 4.1). |
