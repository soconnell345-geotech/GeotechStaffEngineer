# Schedules against plans: North Yard Annex drawings, issue 1

## Door schedule (A-601) against A-101, A-102 and A-401

| Door | Problem |
|---|---|
| **109** | **On a plan but not scheduled.** It is tagged on A-101 (lobby into the core, on grid B) and again on A-401 view 1 (the door into Stair 1). A-601 has no entry for it, so it has no size, frame, fire rating or hardware set. |
| **204** | **Scheduled but not on a plan.** A-601 lists it (OFFICE, HW-2), but A-102 shows only doors 201, 202, 203, 205 and 206. No door is drawn at the 204 position (between 203 and 205 in the office wall on grid 2). |

The rest agree in number and room: 101 to 108 on A-101 and 201, 202, 203, 205 and 206 on A-102 are each scheduled, under the room the plan shows.

## Footing schedule (S-501) against the foundation plan (S-101)

| Mark | Problem |
|---|---|
| **F4** | **On the plan but not scheduled.** It is at grid C/2 and drawn about 2600 x 2600 mm. The schedule lists F1, F2 and F3 only, so F4 has no thickness or reinforcing. |

F1, F2 and F3 are all used on the plan: F1 at the four corners, F2 at B/1, C/1, A/2, D/2, B/3 and C/3, and F3 at B/2. Their drawn sizes match the schedule (1500, 1800 and 2200 mm square). No scheduled mark is missing from the plan.
