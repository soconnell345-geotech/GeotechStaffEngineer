# Levels and grid dimensions: North Yard Annex drawings, issue 1

There are two disagreements.

1. **Second floor level: A-301 against A-201 and S-201.**
   - A-301 gives SECOND FLOOR **EL. 104.50** in both building sections (1/A-301 and 2/A-301).
   - A-201 gives **EL. 104.20** in both elevations, and S-201 gives "SECOND FLOOR - T.O. SLAB EL. **104.20**".
   - A-301's level line is drawn at the same height as A-201's: 4.20 m above the first floor at 1:100. So the 104.50 label is the one in error.

2. **Grid B to C: A-401 against every other plan.**
   - Both enlarged core plans on A-401 (1/A-401 and 2/A-401) dimension B to C as **7500**. That figure is clouded as revision 1.
   - A-101, A-102, S-101, S-201 and S-202 all dimension B to C as **7200**.
   - The A-401 grid lines are still drawn 7200 apart: 288 mm at 1:25.
   - The cover's gross floor area of 415 m2 (2 x 19.2 m x 10.8 m) also implies 7200.
   - Either the 7500 is wrong, or the revision was made on A-401 only and the other five plans were not updated.

These agree across the set:

- **First floor EL. 100.00:** G-002 datum, C-101, A-201 and A-301.
- **Roof EL. 108.40:** A-201, A-301 and S-202 (T.O. slab).
- **Top of parapet EL. 109.30:** A-201 and A-301.
- **Grids:** A to B 6000, B to C 7200 and C to D 6000, with 1 to 2 and 2 to 3 at 5400. This holds on A-101, A-102, S-101, S-201 and S-202, and the drawn grids scale to those figures.
- **Overall size:** the elevations and sections scale to 19.2 m by 10.8 m.

Top of footing EL. 98.00 appears only on S-101, so there is nothing to compare it with.
