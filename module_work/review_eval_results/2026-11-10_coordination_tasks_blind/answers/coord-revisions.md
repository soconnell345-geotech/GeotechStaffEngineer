The new value also contradicts the geotechnical memorandum, which gives 150 kPa (memo 3.2). If the change is intended, cloud it, add rev 1 to S-001 and update the index, and get the geotechnical engineer to confirm it. Otherwise, restore 150 kPa.
2. **The A-401 change is incomplete or wrong.**

3. **The G-001 index changes are listed in the revision block but not clouded.** This is minor, and many offices don't cloud index updates.
