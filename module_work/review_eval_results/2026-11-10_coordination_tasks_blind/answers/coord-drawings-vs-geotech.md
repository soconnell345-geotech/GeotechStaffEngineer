# Structural drawings against the geotechnical memorandum (G-24-031)

The structural drawings do not fully follow the memo. Two notes contradict it, several of its requirements are not carried onto the drawings, and one footing can't be checked.

## Values that contradict the memo

1. **Bearing pressure (S-001 note 2).**
   - The drawings give an allowable net soil bearing pressure of **200 kPa**.
   - Memo 3.2 gives **150 kPa**, and ties that value to about 25 mm of total settlement.
   - Footings proportioned at 200 kPa have only 75 % of the area that 150 kPa needs. Revise the note and recheck the sizes of F1 to F4.
2. **Footing depth (S-001 note 3).**
   - The drawings say the bottom of exterior footings is to be "not less than **1.2 m** below finished grade".
   - Memo 3.3 requires at least **1.5 m**, for frost and to bear below the fill.
   - The footings as drawn are deep enough. Top of footing is El. 98.00 (S-101) and the footings are 400 to 500 mm thick, so they bear at El. 97.50 to 97.60, about 2.2 m below the existing ground (El. 99.8 at the borings). It is the stated minimum that is wrong.
   - The drawings give no finished grade elevation.

## Memo requirements not carried onto the drawings

3. **Slab on grade (memo 3.6).**
   - S-001 note 9 matches the 150 mm compacted granular base and k = 40 MN/m3.
   - It leaves out "over the dense sand, with all fill removed". The fill is 0.9 m thick (to El. 98.9), so without the note the base could be placed on fill.
4. **Bearing stratum (memo 3.1).**
   - No note says the footings bear on the dense sand, below all fill.
   - At El. 97.50 to 97.60 they do lie in the dense sand (El. 98.9 to 93.3), so the design complies by elevation, but nothing on the drawings states it.
5. **Subgrade inspection (memo 4.1).** No note requires the geotechnical engineer to inspect each footing subgrade before concrete is placed.
6. **Excavations (memo 4.2).** No note requires excavations deeper than 1.2 m to be sloped or shored. The footing excavations are about 2.2 m deep. This is minor: it is often left to the specification or the contractor.

## Can't be checked

7. **F4 at C/2 (S-101)** is not in the footing schedule, so its size and design can't be checked against the memo. Its drawn size, about 2.6 m square, is within the 1.2 to 3.0 m width range for which 150 kPa holds.

## What agrees with the memo

- **Seismic site class:** C (S-001 note 4 and memo 3.5).
- **Design groundwater level:** El. 96.50 (S-001 note 5 and memo 3.4).
- **Footing widths:** F1 to F3 are 1.5, 1.8 and 2.2 m, within the 1.2 to 3.0 m range.
- **Dewatering:** the founding levels are above El. 97.0, so none is expected (memo 4.3).
- **Report references:** G-001, C-101 and S-001 all cite report G-24-031, and C-101 shows borings B-1 to B-4.
