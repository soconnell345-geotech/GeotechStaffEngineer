# Schedules against plans: North Yard Annex full set, issue 1 (40 sheets)

I checked four schedules against the marks on the plans:

- the door schedule (A-601 and A-602) against A-101 to A-105 and A-401;
- the footing schedule (S-501) against S-101;
- the mechanical equipment schedule (M-601) against M-101 to M-105;
- the panel schedule (E-601) against E-101 to E-105.

## On a plan but not scheduled

| Mark | Plan | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Door **109** | A-101 (lobby into the core, grid B) and A-401 view 1 | Not in the door schedule. A-601 runs from 108 to 201. |
| Door **407** | A-104 (meeting room, wall on grid 2) | Not in the door schedule. A-602 runs from 406 to 408. |
| Footing **F4** | S-101 at C/2, drawn about 2600 mm square | The footing schedule lists F1, F2 and F3 only. |
| **FCU-3-4** | M-103 (third floor, A–B / 2–3) | M-601 lists FCU-3-1 to FCU-3-3 only. Every other floor has an FCU-n-4 scheduled. |
| Panel **LP-4B** | E-104 (fourth floor, beside LP-4A) | E-601 lists LP-4A only for the fourth floor. Every other floor has both an A and a B panel scheduled. |

## Scheduled but not on a plan

| Mark | Schedule | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Door **204** (OFFICE, HW-2) | A-601 | A-102 tags only 201, 202, 203, 205 and 206, and no door is drawn at the 204 position. |
| Door **509** (CORE, 90 MIN, HW-4) | A-602 | A-105 tags only 501 to 508. No core door is drawn on the fifth floor. |

## What agrees

- **Doors:** 101 to 108, 201 to 203, 205 to 206, 301 to 308, 401 to 406, 408 and 501 to 508 are each on both a plan and the schedule, and the rooms agree.
- **Footings:** F1, F2 and F3 are all used on S-101, and their drawn sizes match the schedule.
- **Mechanical equipment:** AHU-1 is on M-101, and all the other FCUs (FCU-1-1 to 1-4, 2-1 to 2-4, 3-1 to 3-3, 4-1 to 4-4 and 5-1 to 5-4) are on their floor's plan.
- **Panels:** MDP, LP-1A, LP-1B, LP-2A, LP-2B, LP-3A, LP-3B, LP-4A, LP-5A and LP-5B are each on their floor's plan.
