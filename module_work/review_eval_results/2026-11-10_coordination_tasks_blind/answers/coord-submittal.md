# Submittal 03 30 00-02: Class C concrete mix (Mix No. C-35-20) against Section 03 30 00

**Recommendation: revise and resubmit.** The mix exceeds two limits, the strength record is too short, a required item is missing, and the transmittal declares no deviations when there are several.

## Does not comply

| # | Item | Submitted | Required by 03 30 00 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Water-cementitious ratio | **0.48** (213 / 443 kg/m3) | **0.45 maximum** for Class C (2.2) |
| 2 | Fly ash content | **30 %** of cementitious (133 / 443 kg/m3) | **25 % maximum** by mass (2.1) |
| 3 | Strength record | **12** consecutive tests, and no trial-batch results | **At least 30** consecutive tests, or laboratory trial-batch results (1.3 B) |
| 4 | Contractor's review | "DEVIATIONS: NONE" | Items 1 to 3 are deviations. Under 01 33 00, 1.2, every deviation must be marked on the transmittal, and a submittal with unmarked deviations will be returned. |

## Missing

5. **Aggregate test reports (1.3 D).** These are grading, and alkali-silica reactivity by the accelerated mortar-bar method, not more than 12 months old. The transmittal attaches only the mix design, strength record, mill certificate and admixture data.

## Queries

6. **Possible gap in the strength record.** The test dates are 4 days apart throughout, except between test 6 (2025-09-23) and test 7 (2025-10-03), which are 10 days apart. Confirm that no results were left out, because the specification calls for consecutive tests.
7. **Air content.** The mix is submitted as not air-entrained, for interior concrete. That complies with 2.4 only if no Class C columns or suspended slabs are exposed to freezing and thawing. If any are, they need 5 to 8 % air.

## Complies

- Specified strength of 35 MPa, which is the Class C requirement (2.2).
- Portland cement Type GU (2.1). The mill certificate is provided: Type GU, February 2026 production.
- 20 mm coarse aggregate (2.1).
- Slump of 100 mm, within 100 plus or minus 25 mm (2.3).
- Mix proportions are given for every material (1.3 A).
- Admixture product data is provided (1.3 E). The dosage of 1.3 L/m3 works out to 0.29 L per 100 kg of cementitious material, within the product's 0.2 to 0.5 range.
- The record's stated average of 40.9 MPa checks against the 12 results.
