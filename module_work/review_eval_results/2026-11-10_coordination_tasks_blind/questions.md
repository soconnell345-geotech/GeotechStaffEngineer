# Review questions

Each question is asked of the documents listed under it.

## coord-sheet-index

Documents: north_yard_annex_drawings_issue_1.pdf

Check the sheet index on the cover sheet against the sheets that are actually in this set. What does not match?

## coord-callouts

Documents: north_yard_annex_drawings_issue_1.pdf

Check the section, detail and enlarged-plan callouts in this set: does each one point to a view that exists on the sheet it names? List the ones that do not.

## coord-schedules

Documents: north_yard_annex_drawings_issue_1.pdf

Check the schedules against the plans: the door schedule against the door numbers on the floor plans, and the footing schedule against the footing marks on the foundation plan. What is on a plan but not scheduled, and what is scheduled but not on a plan?

## coord-levels-dimensions

Documents: north_yard_annex_drawings_issue_1.pdf

Check that the floor levels and the grid dimensions agree from sheet to sheet: plans, elevations, sections, enlarged plans and structural plans. Where do they disagree?

## coord-drawings-vs-manual

Documents: north_yard_annex_drawings_issue_1.pdf, north_yard_annex_project_manual.pdf

Check the notes on the drawings against the project manual. List any conflict between them, and any reference to the manual that does not resolve.

## coord-drawings-vs-geotech

Documents: north_yard_annex_drawings_issue_1.pdf, geotechnical_recommendations_memorandum.pdf

Do the structural drawings follow the foundation recommendations of the geotechnical memorandum? List every difference.

## coord-revisions

Documents: north_yard_annex_drawings_issue_0.pdf, north_yard_annex_drawings_issue_1.pdf

These are two issues of the same drawing set. What changed in issue 1? For each change, say whether it is clouded and listed in the sheet's revision block.

## coord-submittal

Documents: north_yard_annex_project_manual.pdf, submittal_03_30_00-02_concrete_mix.pdf

Review this concrete mix submittal against the specification section it is submitted under. What does not comply, and what is missing?

## coord-full-review

Documents: north_yard_annex_drawings_issue_1.pdf, north_yard_annex_project_manual.pdf, geotechnical_recommendations_memorandum.pdf

Do a coordination review of this drawing set before it goes out, and check it against the project manual and the geotechnical memorandum. Give me a comment log: one row per comment, with the sheet and what is wrong.
