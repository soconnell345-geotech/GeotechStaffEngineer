said so in `variables`: Hc in Eq. 7-10 (p. 7-39) and Eq. 8-16 (p. 8-45).

### Equation numbers tagged (item 3)

A scan of every printed page's text layer for an equation number standing at
the end of a line found five the text did not mark; each was looked at on
the rendered page and mended by a manifest `fix`:

- **6-12** (p. 6-9) and **7-11** (p. 7-41): the equation was dropped as
  figure lettering (the page prints a figure caption); typed back in with
  its marker.
- **9-21** (p. 9-83) and **9-50** (p. 9-156): the equation came out as
  scattered glyphs ("p 8 = s", "ft (cost /"); replaced by the equation as
  printed, and 9-21's broken where-list ("' N = ... = [ - 1 ≥") by its
  printed lines.
- **9-34b** (p. 9-128): `[Eq. 9-34a]` stood after the 9-34b line and 9-34b
  was a bare number; now `Qu = Qs + Qt – W [Eq. 9-34a]` and
  `Qu ≤ 0.7Qs + W [Eq. 9-34b]`.

**Not printed, so not invented:** 5-11 to 5-13 (the print goes from Eq.
5-10, p. 5-42, to Eq. 5-14, p. 5-62; pages 5-43 to 5-61 looked at, none
numbered), 9-14 (9-13 on p. 9-53, 9-15a on p. 9-60; pp. 9-54 to 9-59 carry
only unnumbered step equations) and 9-27 to 9-33 (9-26b on p. 9-99, 9-34a on
p. 9-128; pp. 9-100 to 9-127 looked at). The item listed these as untagged;
they do not exist in this print. The scan's one other hit, "6-11a)" on
p. 6-19, is Figure 6-11a's label.

The library now lists 205 equation numbers (200 + 5).

## How each was checked

Each page was rendered with PyMuPDF (110 dpi, 160 dpi zooms where a glyph
was in doubt: 9-45a "1.2N60", whose text layer reads "2N60"; 9-42; 10-8 and
10-9) and the formula typed from the image, not from the text layer. The
variables are the page's own "where:" lines; where a symbol is defined
elsewhere on the same or next page (9-47's alpha_E and f'c on p. 9-141;
9-43's limit of 4 ksf) that page is named.

## What changed

- refs `scripts/build_printed_text.py`: the manifest's `printed_equations`
  (per volume, beside `printed_tables`) give each listed equation its
  `formula`, `variables`, optional `notes`, and a `source` line; an entry's
  `fix` (`{"find", "replace"}` or a list) mends body text where the text
  layer lost or misplaced an equation. The build raises if a fix finds no
  text or a formula is attached to no section, so a change in the extractor
  cannot drop one silently. `build_chapters()` builds without writing;
  `--text-only` writes the chapter text and leaves `figures_catalog.json`
  alone.
- refs `scripts/manifests/fhwa_soils_foundations.json`: 14 + 38 entries.
- refs `scripts/chapter_schema.json`: the four optional equation fields.
- refs chapter files 2, 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10: regenerated by
  `python scripts/build_printed_text.py fhwa_soils_foundations --text-only`.
  No section split, moved or was renamed (section counts per chapter
  unchanged); the largest section is still 6.6.3 at 13,978 characters (the
  limit the tests hold is 14,000); the largest section given a formula
  is 8.4.2 at 9,415.
- app `funhouse_agent/tests/test_fhwa_soils_foundations_reference.py`: two
  tests (below).

No calculation module changed; no calculated value changes.
